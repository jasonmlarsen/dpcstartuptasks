# Signup and onboarding: tailoring the list without adding friction

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01, 02
Map: ../map.md

## Question

Deliberately lower priority than the tickets above — but it shapes the first impression, which for a free product is most of the conversion.

Decided in principle: ask a few skippable questions at registration and pre-mark irrelevant tasks N/A, showing the physician what was set aside and how to turn it back on. Still open:

1. **What is the question set?** Candidates: which state, mobile vs. fixed location, hiring staff in year one, opting out of Medicare. Each question must retire enough tasks to justify its own friction — a question that hides two tasks is not worth asking.
2. **How does `state_specific` actually work?** The CSV flags tasks as state-specific but carries no per-state content. Does collecting the state do anything beyond a warning label, and is per-state content a future ambition or a dead end?
3. **Where does this sit in the flow?** Before the magic link, after first login, or a dismissible banner on the list itself? Anything between "I want to try this" and "I can see the list" costs signups.
4. **Skipping.** What a skipper sees — the full 106 — and how they discover tailoring later.
5. **Changing answers later.** A practice that starts mobile and signs a lease needs those 14 tasks back without losing anything.

## Notes for the session

This is a product-shaping conversation, not a technical one. Resolve the task list screen (02) first so there is something concrete to tailor.
